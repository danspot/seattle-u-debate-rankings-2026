How rankings, stars, and missing scores work

Speaker rank: the mean of actual numeric scores in Rounds 1 and 2, highest first. A one-round average is marked provisional and participates in the available-score ranking. No score is assigned for a BYE or missing ballot. Speakers without numeric scores are unranked. Tied averages share competition ranks (1, 2, 2, 4); alphabetical order only arranges tied rows. Searching and sorting never change ranks.

Room honors: ★ means the speaker had the highest individual score in their debate in exactly one round; ★★ means both rounds. Everyone tied for the highest score receives credit. All scores in a debate must be available to verify its highest score. If another round is missing, a single verified room high is described as pending rather than awarding a potentially incorrect star.

Team standings: completed records are grouped 2–0, 1–1, then 0–2, with shared competition standings. Alphabetical order is not a tournament tiebreaker. A BYE counts as a win only when both the results styling and ballot explicitly identify the team as the winner; its “B” speaker marks remain unscored. Unknown results and missing pairings stay incomplete.

Source fidelity: team names, speaker names, and scores come only from the linked Plustab results and rendered ballots. Source roster labels such as “ABC” are retained, marked, and kept distinct by team. Member names for abbreviated teams come from their ballots. If a ballot is unavailable, we do not guess its roster. Winners are read from computed underlining/font weight, then checked against ballot decisions. Judges’ written comments are not collected in the snapshot or published.

This is an independent two-round snapshot, not official tournament speaker awards or final standings. It makes no adjustments for judge scoring differences. Updates are manual; this page makes no live tournament requests.
