Scoring & data

Averages use available numeric scores. Incomplete averages and records show the number of available rounds (for example, 2/3). Tied averages share ranks (1, 2, 2, 4). Teams with the same record share a standing and are listed alphabetically. Ranks cover all selected divisions and stay fixed when searching. These are independent rankings for Rounds 1–3.

Blue stars mark the highest score on a speaker’s own team; gold stars mark the highest across both teams in the room. Each set counts qualifying rounds, including ties. Missing scores leave that comparison unverified; a team high can still be verified when only an opponent’s score is missing. BYEs earn no stars. “B” is not a score. Source roster labels such as “ABC” are retained.
